Report something privately.

01

Start a DM

Open Gargand's Discord profile and send report. You can also use !report in a DM.

02

Describe what happened

Send one message. For the clearest review, use server | person or target | what happened. You can use Gargand for a bot or website issue.

03

Staff review it

Gargand sends a receipt with a report number. Owners can review the report and update its status in the private website admin panel.

YOUR PRIVACY

The report details are visible to Gargand's authorized website administrators and are not posted to a public Discord channel. The report stores your Discord ID so staff can investigate abuse and follow up. Do not include passwords, tokens or other account secrets.
